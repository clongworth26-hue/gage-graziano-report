Assessment & Development Plan

Scouting Read Burkey

  • Ceiling: Command-first back-end starter / long-man / sinker-slider middle reliever — if velo ticks ~92-93 mph with added mass + third pitch materializes.
  • Median: D1 depth arm / undrafted-signing / indy ball.
  • No plus pitch today. FB = contact pitch (ground balls, 4.5-8.3% whiff). SL = best miss (15.4%) but still below pro put-away.
  • High-spin trap SL ~2500 rpm → ~3" IVB/−2" HB. Spin isn't converting to break efficiently. Verify spin efficiency before pricing the slider.
  • Competition flag vs WCU: zone 48.6%→33.3%, strike 59.5%→41.7%. Leaned harder on slider against better hitters. Command/confidence signal.

Dev Plan Coach Chad

  1. Physical mass + arm conditioning — 160 lb at 5'10"/19 is the cap. Lean to 175-185 = +3-4 mph free FB velo + endurance. Highest leverage, lowest regret.
  2. FB command under competition — the intersquad→WCU drop is the real tell. Live ABs, sequenced pens, both-side command, mechanics video under fatigue.
  3. Changeup rebuild — currently a non-pitch (0% zone, 2x/outing, tight ~6 mph gap). Deeper grip + full arm speed now; frame gains widen gap long-term. Target 10-12 mph separation, 15-20% usage.
  4. Leave the slider alone — it's the one thing that works (76.9% strike). Refine shape/command but don't over-tinker.
  5. Velo floor: Pull if FB < 88 mph. Track intra-outing velo on TrackMan. Current 1.7 mph fade over 37 pitches = yellow, not red; real test at 60-80 pitches.

Single Highest-Leverage Move

Commit to the changeup. It's the only lever that changes his tier — right now he's a RHH-only sinker/slider arm with nothing that fades LHH and a ghost third pitch. Get it to 78-80 mph with zone command and 15-20% usage, and he becomes a three-pitch starter-mix instead of a ROOGY-ish reliever. The raw material (1597 rpm kill-spin trait) is correct — the velo gap and trust are the missing links.

Fallback if CH never comes: add 3-4 mph off frame gain and sharpen SL into true swing-and-miss by chasing spin efficiency.
